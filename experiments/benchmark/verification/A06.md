## Flawed version

1. **Verbatim quotes.** All 4 quotes are found exactly once. None is in Limitations, and none appears in the clean file.
2. **Planted flaws:**
   - F1 (S2): L3.H9 has Δtop-1 = −0.003 and ΔCE = 0.006 nats, against a 0.004-nat median across all 720 combinations. Its q = 4e-9 comes from 2,310 cells, and it is read as a "stemness head". The flaw is real and detectable from the text.
   - F2 (C1): the sevenfold erythroid-vs-other contrast (0.41 vs 0.06 nats) compares genes at median input rank 9 with genes at rank 512. No rank-, expression- or detection-matched controls are used. The flaw is real.
   - F3 (L1): "L7.H4 is the circuit through which Geneformer implements GATA1-driven regulation… reads the GATA1 and KLF1 tokens and uses that signal". Whole-head ablation plus attention weights do not establish this route. The flaw is real.
   - F4 (B1): MYOD1, a skeletal-muscle master TF, is listed as a core erythroid regulator. It would not even be a detected token in erythroid cells. The flaw is real and the criterion is specific.
3. **No unplanned flaws found.**
   - BH correction is applied over all 720 head×lineage tests, with replication in held-out donors.
   - Gene sets were fixed before any model run, and the dataset is not in the pretraining corpus.
   - Attention is described correctly: query rows put mass on keys, and a head's output is the attention-weighted sum of values before the output projection.
   - Erythroid markers, HSC markers and GATA1/KLF1/TAL1 are all correct.
   - Using cells rather than donors as the test unit is common for deterministic-model analyses and is not one of the 12 types.
4. **Model facts are correct.** V2-104M has 12 × 12 = 144 heads, 768 d and 64 d per head. Pretraining is masked-gene with 15% masking on rank-value input.
5. **Numbers are consistent.**
   - Cell counts sum to 41,600 (20,500 + 21,100).
   - 144 × 5 lineages = 720 tests.
   - 0.412 − 0.349 = 0.063 and 0.284 − 0.281 = 0.003, matching the Δtop-1 column.
   - 0.41 / 0.06 ≈ 6.8, reported as "sevenfold".
   - The q-values are plausible given the CI widths and cell numbers.
   - The TF attention shares add up to 28.5% of the 31% total.
6. **No meta-commentary.**

## Clean version

- All four flaws are fixed:
  - A 0.05-nat practical-relevance threshold, with L3.H9 called negligible.
  - Controls matched on input rank (±3) and detection decile, plus a regression. The erythroid excess is 0.41 − 0.22 = 0.19 nats, and the CI of 0.15–0.23 fits the component CIs. Rank and detection explain (0.35 − 0.19) / 0.35 ≈ 46%, reported as "about half".
  - The attention finding is stated as an association, with a proposed test that blocks attention to TF keys.
  - NFE2 replaces MYOD1.
- "Only three exceeded the threshold" is consistent with Table 1 and with the 0.004-nat median.
- No new instance of any of the 12 types was found.
- **Length:** clean 1,095 raw words vs flawed 968 (+13%).

## GT fix

- The A06-F1 detection criterion previously required all three of these: that the effect is negligible, that the significance comes from the large number of cells, and that "stemness head" is unwarranted. A critique that correctly calls the 0.003 / 0.006-nat effect negligible and rejects the stemness-head reading, but does not mention sample size, would have been scored as a miss.
- The criterion now requires singling out L3.H9, saying its effect is practically negligible despite the tiny q, and rejecting the "stemness head" interpretation. The large-N explanation and the comparison with the 0.004-nat median are accepted as supporting examples. Generic effect-size remarks and remarks about L7.H4 are still excluded.
