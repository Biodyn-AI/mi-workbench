## Checks

1. **Verbatim quotes.** All 4 quotes occur exactly once in the artifact (checked with Python after the edits). None of them appears in the Limitations section.
2. **Planted flaws:**
   - F1 (S1): 412 per-TF Wilcoxon tests, hits called at p < 0.05 with no correction. The number of tests is stated twice. About 20.6 of the 87 hits are expected by chance. The flaw is real and detectable, and the criterion is strict.
   - F2 (C1): the deletion shift is read as the "regulatory weight". The random control is not matched on rank (median rank 104 for hits vs 918 for controls, both stated in the text). Under rank-value encoding this confound is real, and the criterion excludes generic complaints about "more controls" or "not a knockout".
   - F3 (L2): the DE "confirmation" uses all 38 donors (per Methods), and the disease axis is built from NF-vs-DCM differences in those same donors. Concordance is expected by construction. The flaw is real.
   - F4 (B2): REST/NRSF represses NPPA/NPPB and the fetal gene program in adult ventricular myocytes. Calling it an activator reverses the sign. The flaw is real and the criterion is specific.
3. **No unplanned flaws found.** Axis donors and screening donors are disjoint. Donors are the unit of replication. DE is BH-corrected. Effect sizes are reported. The biological claims (GATA4, TBX20, MEF2A, NKX2-5, and ESRRG activating OXPHOS/FAO genes) are correct. Deletion is an intervention on the model, so the model-level wording does not count as L1. Candidates are framed as hypotheses for knockdown experiments.
4. **Model facts are correct.** V2-104M has 12 layers, 12 heads and 768 d. Pretraining is masked-gene. Rank-value encoding normalizes by total counts and by each gene's non-zero median. Input is 4,096 tokens. Penultimate layer = 11.
5. **Numbers are consistent.**
   - 22 − 8 = 14 screening donors, and 14 × 1,000 = 14,000 nuclei.
   - The exact one-sided Wilcoxon p for 14/14 donors is 1/2^14 = 6.1e-5. For 13/14 it is 2/2^14 = 1.2e-4.
   - Fisher's test on [[62,25],[69,256]] gives OR 9.20 and p = 7.5e-18. Both match the text.
   - 3.4 / 0.9 ≈ 3.8. 412 − 87 = 325.
6. **No meta-commentary. Word count 1,083** (raw `wc -w`).
