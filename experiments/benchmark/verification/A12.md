# Verification: A12 (Geneformer V2-104M gene-embedding cosine vs STRING physical PPIs, Tabula Sapiens)

Files: `artifacts/A12-flawed.md`, `ground_truth/A12-flawed.json`. This item has no clean counterpart.

Verdict: FIXED. The artifact numbers were adjusted for internal consistency, and the ground-truth quote and criterion were updated to match.

## Checks
1. **Quotes.** All 4 quotes match the artifact verbatim, and each occurs exactly once (re-checked after the edit). F1 and F3 are in Results, F2 in Methods, and F4 in Interpretation. None is in Limitations.
2. **Planted flaws.**
   - F1 S2: the mean cosine differs by 0.007 against SDs of about 0.14 (d ≈ 0.05), and p = 1e-29 is driven by 51,890 vs 33.7M pairs. The result is read as partners being "systematically closer". Valid. The z recomputes to 11.3, which gives p ≈ 1e-29.
   - F2 T2: Geneformer is described as a next-token model, and the hidden state at i − 1 is used as gene i's embedding. Geneformer is actually a masked-gene, bidirectional encoder, so the error invalidates the embeddings. It is load-bearing and valid.
   - F3 L2: in-silico deletion in the same Geneformer model is called "independent confirmation" of candidate PPIs. The check uses the same model representations, so it is circular. Valid.
   - F4 C1: the text claims that restricting STRING to its physical subnetwork rules out co-expression. It has no co-expression or expression baseline on the same cells, and the top hits are ribosome, complex I, proteasome, CCT and MCM, which are highly expressed, co-regulated complexes. Valid. The C2 angle is handled correctly by the degree-preserving null, so this is cleanly C1.
   - None of the flaws is hedged or signposted. The Limitations entries (healthy donors, averaging over contexts, one model or layer, STRING transfers from other organisms) do not touch the flaws.
3. **Unplanned flaws.** None found.
   - The top-k precision uses a degree-preserving null (no C2).
   - AUPRC is reported with its base rate (no S3).
   - Layer 11 was fixed in advance (no C3).
   - "Tracks" is associational wording.
   - The complex subunit counts are within the possible maxima (CCT 24 of 28 pairs, MCM 14 of 15).
4. **Model facts.** Apart from F2 everything is correct: V2-104M has 12 layers, 12 heads and 768 d, uses rank-value encoding with normalization by the non-zero median and a 4,096-token context, and layer 11 is the penultimate layer.
5. **Clean counterpart.** Not present for this item.
6. **Internal consistency.**

   - 1,318 / 10,000 = 13.2%, which is 86-fold over the base rate.
   - AUPRC 0.0093 is 6.0× the base rate.

7. **Meta-commentary.** None.

## Fixes made
- A12-flawed Results: the mean cosines were changed from 0.084 and 0.077 to 0.019 and 0.012. The SDs (0.141, 0.139), the 0.007 difference and p = 1e-29 are unchanged. The Methods say embeddings were mean-centred across genes "to remove the shared component that otherwise makes all cosines positive", so a residual mean cosine of about 0.08 was inconsistent. With mean 0.077 and SD 0.139 the top-10,000 cutoff (the 99.97th percentile) would also be about 0.55, which contradicts the "Other" median of 0.49.
- A12-flawed Table 1: the "Other" median cosine was changed from 0.49 to 0.51, so that it sits above the implied top-10,000 cutoff (about 0.49 after the fix above).
- ground_truth/A12-flawed.json F1: the quote and detection criterion were updated to "0.019 ... 0.012" and "(0.019 vs 0.012, ...)". The description (0.007 difference, SD about 0.14, d about 0.05, AUROC about 0.51) still holds.

## Residual concerns
- A reviewer may also describe F4's wording ("reflects protein–protein interaction itself") as causal overclaiming (L1). The F4 criterion requires co-expression or expression confounding to be named, so a pure L1 framing would not match. This is acceptable, because the confound is the substantive error.
- Tabula Sapiens is likely in Geneformer's pretraining corpus. This is not leakage for a STRING-based evaluation (the labels are not part of pretraining), so no change was made.
