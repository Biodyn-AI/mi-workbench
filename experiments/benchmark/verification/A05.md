# A05 verification (SAE features on the scGPT residual stream vs curated pathways)

Files: `artifacts/A05-flawed.md`, `artifacts/A05-clean.md`, `ground_truth/A05-flawed.json`, `ground_truth/A05-clean.json`.

Verdict: FIXED (the clean version had an internal numerical impossibility; one arithmetic slip in the GT)

## Flawed version

1. **Verbatim quotes.** All 4 quotes are found exactly once. None is in Limitations, and none appears in the clean file.
2. **Planted flaws:**
   - F1 (S1): alignment is called from the best uncorrected p < 0.01 over 1,665 sets per feature (7,236 × 1,665 = 12,047,940 tests). The text itself reports a median random-set best −log10 p of 2.8, so most random lists would pass. The flaw is detectable.
   - F2 (C2): the uniform random-gene-set null has no matching on annotation degree or study bias, while the top genes are dominated by hub genes (RP, OXPHOS, HLA, ISG). The flaw is real, and the criterion requires the degree/annotation-bias argument.
   - F3 (T2): "scGPT orders each cell's genes by expression rank" is false. scGPT uses value binning, and token order carries no expression information. The error drives the choice of the "first 512 = top-expressed genes". The flaw is real and matters for the analysis.
   - F4 (B2): HLA-DR/DP/DQ and CD74 (MHC class II, presenting to CD4+ T cells) are labelled as MHC class I / CD8+. This is pathway misattribution, and the criterion is specific.
3. **No unplanned flaws found.**
   - The SAE is trained on donors disjoint from the characterization donors.
   - Hyperparameters were chosen on a validation split of the training donors.
   - The atlas was generated after the pretraining snapshot, so there is no C3 problem.
   - Expression-level features were set aside.
   - The write-up explicitly declines causal claims ("would require ablating or steering them").
   - Limitation 3 (uneven Reactome/Hallmark coverage) concerns under-annotation, not the degree bias of the null. Acceptable.
4. **Model facts are correct** apart from the planted F3. scGPT whole-human has 12 layers, 8 heads, 512 d and about 33M cells. The TopK SAE has 8,192 = 16 × 512 features.
5. **Numbers are consistent.**
   - 174,300 + 37,700 = 212,000.
   - 254 / 8,192 = 3.1%. 7,938 − 702 = 7,236.
   - 6,981 / 7,236 = 96.5%, which fits a best-p median of 10^-8.7 with IQR from 10^-4.9 upward.
   - The Table 1 hypergeometric p-values are within about 1 order of magnitude of values recomputed from the stated fold enrichments, assuming set sizes of 85–180 in the background.

## Clean version

- **Problem found:** the clean version reported 47.2% of features aligned after BH, yet also reported a median best-set −log10 p of 8.7 (IQR 4.9–15.2).
  - The strictest possible BH cutoff (Bonferroni) over 12,047,940 tests is p = 4.15e-9, or −log10 8.38. More than half of the features therefore clear even Bonferroni.
  - At p < 1e-8, the fold ≥ 3 and overlap ≥ 4 filters are always met.
  - An aligned fraction below 50% was therefore impossible.
  - The matched null showed the same problem: 6.9% aligned with a median of 4.6, against a realistic BH cutoff near 10^-3.7.
  - A careful reviewer could flag this as an internal inconsistency.
- **Fix:** Results and Interpretation were rewritten with a coherent set of numbers:
  - BH corresponds to a nominal cutoff of p ≤ 1.9e-4. This implies about 45,800 rejections, or about 8 significant sets per aligned feature, which is plausible.
  - 5,818 / 7,236 features (80.4%) are aligned.
  - The matched null aligns 41.7% (median best −log10 p 3.9, IQR 3.0–5.1). The uniform null aligns 1.8% (median 2.8, unchanged).
  - The excess is 38.7 percentage points (95% bootstrap CI 37.2–40.2).
  - The matched null reproduces about half of the features' advantage in alignment rate over uniform lists: (41.7 − 1.8) / (80.4 − 1.8) = 0.51.
  - The Interpretation now says that alignment beyond annotation/expression bias is substantial but far from universal, and that a uniform null would overstate it about twofold (78.6 / 38.7 = 2.0).
  - The shared numbers (Table 1, the SAE feature median) are unchanged.
- All four flaws are fixed:
  - BH correction over the stated 12,047,940 tests, plus fold and overlap requirements.
  - A null matched on annotation degree and expression.
  - A correct description of value binning (51 bins), with 1,200 genes sampled at random.
  - MHC class II / CD4+, with IFN-γ induction via CIITA.
- No new instance of any of the 12 types was found. Claims are phrased as associations.
- **Length:** clean 1,097 raw words vs flawed 897 (+22%, within the 25% limit).
