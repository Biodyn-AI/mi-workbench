DoRothEA A-C contains only about 429 TFs in total (counted in the local `dorothea_human.tsv`: 32,272 A-C edges, 429 TFs), so 431 is impossible and 402 at 5% detection is implausible. Changed the counts to 301 TFs x 9,236 genes (kidney) and 318 TFs x 9,720 genes (lung), with a 2% detection threshold and "candidate target gene" wording. The pair totals (2.78M and 3.09M), reference-edge counts and 0.34% base rates are unchanged, so no downstream number changes.
