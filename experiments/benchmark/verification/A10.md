## Fixes made
- The universe was defined as genes detected in at least 1% of cells, and each sample needed at least 20 of up to 400 cells to contain the gene. Under those rules, the reported 94% of universe genes meeting the scoring requirement (3,655 of 3,904 and 3,263 of 3,466) is implausible, because genes near 1% detection average about 4 cells per sample. The universe is now "detected in at least 5% of its cells" and the per-sample requirement is "at least 10 cells". The word count is unchanged (1,096).

The rank-position adjustment and the co-expression comparison make this a weak, secondary point rather than a clear unplanned instance, so it was left.
